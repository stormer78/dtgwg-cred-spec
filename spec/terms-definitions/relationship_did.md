[[def: R-DID, relationship DID, R-DIDs]]

~ **Retired as a normative type.** Informal shorthand for an identifier a party uses with one counterparty in a peer-to-peer [trust relationship](https://glossary.trustoverip.org/#term:trust-relationship). Normatively this is an identifier whose declared [[ref: correlation scope]] is `pairwise`; the relationship itself is established by the [[ref: VRC]] the identifier appears in, not by the identifier's name. Retained because the abbreviation is established in working-group discussion, and because an identifier declared `pairwise` and then used with a second counterparty simply is not pairwise — a property the type name could only assert, and the scope declaration makes definitional.
