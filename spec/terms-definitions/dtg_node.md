[[def: DTG node, DTG nodes]]

~ In a [[ref: DTG]], a DTG node represents an entity that participates in [trust relationships](https://glossary.trustoverip.org/#term:trust-relationship) with other DTG nodes. DTG node types include persons, devices, AI agents, [[ref: VTCs]], [[ref: VTNs]], and services — a service being an operated endpoint that holds its own identifier and participates in trust relationships in its own right, such as a message mediator, a DID host, or a trust registry, as distinct from the [[ref: VTSP]] that operates it. The list is illustrative rather than closed: what makes something a DTG node is that it holds a [[ref: DTG verifiable identifier]] and forms [[ref: DTG edges]], not membership of an enumerated set. A DTG node is always identified by at least one [[ref: DTG verifiable identifier]] (VID) and can be interacted with via a [[ref: VTA]].
