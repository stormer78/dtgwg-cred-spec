[[def: VRC, verifiable relationship credential, VRCs, verifiable relationship credentials]]

~ A [[ref: DTG edge credential]] that represents one half of a bidirectional peer-to-peer [trust relationship](https://glossary.trustoverip.org/#term:trust-relationship) in a [[ref: DTG]]. Each peer chooses the identifier it appears under and the [[ref: correlation scope]] it declares for it; the two halves need not agree. A VRC is only verifiable as a [[ref: DTG edge]] in the context of a specific [[ref: VTN]] if: a) each peer is identified by an identifier that is the subject of a [[ref: VMC]] issued by a [[ref: VTC]], b) both peers have signed the VRC using the corresponding private keys, and c) those VTCs are recognized as [[ref: VTN trust anchors]] in that VTN.
