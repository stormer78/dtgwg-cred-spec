[[def: C-DID, community DID, C-DIDs]]

~ **Retired as a normative type.** Informal shorthand for the identifier of a [[ref: VTC]] or [[ref: VTN]]. Normatively it is an identifier whose declared [[ref: correlation scope]] is `public`: published and meant to be found. A community's identifier is not a fourth kind of thing — it is a public-scoped identifier that happens to be held by a community.
