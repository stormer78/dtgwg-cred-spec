[[def: VTC member, VTC members]]

~ A person or any other type of [[ref: DTG node]] that has accepted a [[ref: VMC]] from a [[ref: VTC]] by issuing the reverse VMC that completes the membership edge. If the new member is another VTC, it is identified using its `public` identifier; otherwise the new member is identified using one declared `community` for that VTC. A common pattern (depending on VTC governance) is for existing VTC members to invite new members by issuing them a [[ref: VTC invitation credential]].
