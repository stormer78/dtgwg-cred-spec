[[def: VPC, verifiable persona credential, VPCs, verifiable persona credentials]]

~ A [[ref: DTG annotation credential]] that enables one party to a [[ref: DTG edge]] relationship to assert a [[ref: persona]] to the counterparty. The issuer of the VPC is identified with a `linked`-scoped identifier. The counterparty (holder) can now prove that the counterparty knows the issuer in the context of that persona. Persona identifiers enable individuals to control intentional correlation separately from the identifiers by which they are known to a community.
