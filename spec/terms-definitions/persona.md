[[def: persona, personas]]

~ An [identity](https://glossary.trustoverip.org/#term:identity) controlled by the person it identifies (or their [delegate](https://glossary.trustoverip.org/#term:delegation)) in order to manage [correlation privacy](https://glossary.trustoverip.org/#term:correlation-privacy). In a [[ref: DTG]], a persona has a unique [verifiable identifier](https://glossary.trustoverip.org/#term:verifiable-identifier), declared with a `linked` [[ref: correlation scope]], that may be shared using a [[ref: VPC]].

~ A member's identifier can be considered a specialized form of persona that is required for membership in a [[ref: VTC]] — one whose [[ref: correlation scope]] is `community`. By contrast, a persona identifier is declared `linked`: its sharing is completely under the control of the asserting person.
